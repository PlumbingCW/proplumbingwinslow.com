While you wait for the plumber

  1. Stop the waterTurn the main shutoff clockwise. If you cannot find it, turn off the valve at the leaking fixture.
  2. Stop using drainsA backing-up line gets worse with every flush, load of laundry or running tap.
  3. Watch the electricsKeep people and cords away from standing water, and leave the breaker panel alone if the floor is wet.

Winslow emergencies

What an emergency plumber in Winslow actually deals with

Winslow Township covers 58 square miles of Camden County, and the housing varies a lot from one corner to the next.

Sicklerville and the Winslow Crossing area grew fast in the 1970s when Levitt & Sons built thousands of homes there, so many houses now have plumbing past the age where a repipe starts to make sense. If a supply line has already split, burst pipe repair is the first call.

Water comes from the Township Department of Municipal Utilities, which draws on wells in the Cohansey and Mount Laurel-Wenonah aquifers and buys some water from New Jersey American Water.

The Township's published water report does not list hardness, so we never quote a number. Test your own tap, and if scale is building up in a heater, see water heater repair before the tank fails.

About 80 percent of the township sits inside the Pinelands National Reserve, and the local aquifer is a water-table system that rises and falls with rainfall. After a wet spell a basement in Cedar Brook or Blue Anchor can take on water, and that is when a sump pump gets its first real test.

If the 08095 or 08081 mailing address on your bill confuses you, it does not matter to us. We dispatch to every Winslow ZIP, including 08018, 08004 and the 08037 used in the eastern part of the township.

Sewer service in Winslow runs through the Township and the Camden County Municipal Utilities Authority. A backup that reaches the lowest drain in the house needs sewer line cleaning first, and a camera run through a sewer camera inspection shows whether the pipe itself is cracked.

For hidden wet spots and a water bill that jumped, leak detection starts with the meter.

Plumbing work in New Jersey follows the National Standard Plumbing Code, 2021 edition, and the Township Construction Office decides which jobs need a permit. We will tell you what we know and send you to that office for the rest.

Emergency plumber questions in Winslow, NJ

Yes. The phone line is answered all day and all night, including weekends and holidays. We sort calls by danger to the home, so an active leak or a sewage backup goes ahead of a dripping faucet.

Shut off the water at the main or at the fixture, stop using any drain that is backing up, keep people away from standing water near outlets, and photograph the damage. If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 before you call us.

Ask for the plumbing license number. New Jersey requires licensees to show it on vehicles, letters and advertising, and the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers at njconsumeraffairs.gov/plu is the place to check it.

The Township Department of Municipal Utilities handles the public system up to your meter and curb box. Call 609-567-0700 during office hours. Pipe problems on your side of the meter are plumbing work, and we can help.

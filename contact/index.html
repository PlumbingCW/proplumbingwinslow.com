Public offices that can help

Some problems belong to the Township rather than to a plumber. The Winslow Township Department of Municipal Utilities at 609-567-0700 handles the public water and sewer system. The Construction Office, at the same number on option 5, answers permit questions.

For a gas smell, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911. Do not call a plumber first. See plumbing installation for how we handle gas line work once the utility says it is safe.

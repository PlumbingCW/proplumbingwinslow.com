Which utility serves your street

Sewer service in the township comes from the Gloucester Township Municipal Utilities Authority, whose office is on Lower Landing Road in Chews Landing at 856-227-8666. The Camden County Municipal Utilities Authority is also part of the system. Water comes from two companies, Aqua New Jersey, which operates in Erial, and New Jersey American Water.

You can usually tell who serves you from your bill. If a backup affects the whole street, call the sewer authority at the same time as you call us. If it is only your house, the blockage is probably in the line you own, and that line is ours to clear or camera.

Sewer backups and the lines that cause them

A sewer backup is the call that cannot wait. Water rises in the lowest drain, usually a basement floor drain or a first-floor tub, and every flush pushes more up. Stop using water, keep children and pets out of the area and call. Sewage in a living space is a health hazard, and we will tell you what to protect.

When the blockage is in your own line, sewer line cleaning with a powered cable clears it and restores flow. If roots or grease keep bringing it back, ask about hydro jetting, which scours the pipe wall instead of punching a hole through the clog. We do not recommend jetting a pipe that a camera shows is cracked or collapsed.

What a sewer camera shows

A sewer camera inspection runs a small camera through the line from a cleanout and records what it sees: roots at a joint, a belly where water sits, a crack, a collapsed section. That recording is the basis for an honest decision, and it is the best defense against paying to replace pipe that only needed cleaning.

It also helps if you are buying or selling. Ask for the footage and the notes, and keep them with the house papers.

Three ZIP codes, one large township

Blackwood is 08012, Clementon is 08021 and Glendora is 08029, and Gloucester Township's other communities include Grenloch, Chews Landing, Erial, Hilltop, Blenheim and Lakeland.

Routes 168 and 42 and County Road 534 are the main roads through it, and the Atlantic City Expressway crosses the southwest, which is also how many of these calls connect to Winslow. Calls from Winslow's 08081 and 08095 ZIPs for sewer cleaning and jetting work the same way.

Permits and offices

For permits, ask the Township's construction office, since permit rules vary and the office decides whether a job needs one. New Jersey requires plumbing to be done by a master plumber, an authorized plumbing contractor or their employees.

In Camden County, Berlin Borough is nearby and runs its own water department. Across the county line in Gloucester County, Monroe Township has its own offices and its own authority.

Find the cleanout

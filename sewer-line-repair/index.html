Emergency Sewer Line Repair in Winslow, NJ

A sewer line that has failed can send wastewater into the lowest part of your home, so treat a backup as urgent. Our 24/7 dispatch takes these calls, and while you wait you should stop using water, keep everyone out of the affected area, and shut off power to appliances standing in it.

Sewer line repair in Winslow, NJ means fixing the buried pipe that carries wastewater from your house to the street connection or septic system. The job can be a single spot repair, a lined section, or a full replacement, depending on what the pipe looks like.

Sewer backup: first steps and when it points to a broken line

A clog and a break can look the same at first. Both fill the lowest drain with wastewater. The difference shows after the line is cleared. If the backup returns in days or weeks, or a cable keeps catching at one distance, the pipe itself is suspect.

  • ✓Backups that keep coming back after cleaning
  • ✓A wet, sunken or unusually green patch of yard over the line's path
  • ✓Sewer odor outside near the foundation
  • ✓Gurgling fixtures and slow drains throughout the house
  • ✓A cable that stops at the same point each time

Stop all water use and call. A same-day appointment, when available, is the safest route, because wastewater in a finished basement spreads damage fast. We do not diagnose from the phone alone, so expect a camera look to come first.

Where we start: find the failure before choosing the fix

Repair depends on a camera inspection. We run the camera, mark the distance to each problem and, if needed, trace the line from the surface. That tells us whether we are fixing one section or the whole run, and it shapes your options. For a closer look at that step, see our sewer camera inspection page.

Cast iron, clay and plastic pipe: what it means for you

Older sewer and drain lines can be cast iron, clay or plastic, and each fails in its own way. Cast iron tends to corrode from the inside and flake, clay joints can let roots in, and plastic can crack or sag if bedding settles. The camera tells us what we are dealing with.

We do not assume a material from the age of a house. Winslow sewer service: what the Township and CCMUA control

In the Township, a sewer connection starts with an application to the Department of Municipal Utilities.

The Camden County Municipal Utilities Authority approves a separate application, the Construction Department issues the permit, a registered plumber makes the connection, and the plumbing inspector must approve it before sewer billing starts. The Township's published connection fee is $3,998 plus a $5 application fee.

That matters for repairs because the section on your property is yours to fix, but the connection and the system beyond are the utility's. Call the DMU at 609-567-0700 to ask where your responsibility ends.

The Construction Office, at option 5 on the same number, says whether your repair needs a permit. Plumbing inspections are held Tuesday through Thursday, 8 a.m. to 1 p.m.

New Jersey's plumbing subcode is the 2021 edition of the National Standard Plumbing Code Illustrated, and plumbing work must be done by a licensed master plumber or an authorized contractor.

Ask any plumber for a license number and verify it with the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers. The Pinelands setting of about 80 percent of Winslow also means roots are common, which is why we look for them in yard lines in 08095, 08018 and 08004.

Septic: where our work stops

Some Winslow streets are not on public sewer. If your house uses septic, the repair we do is on the house plumbing side, including the line from the house to the tank. A septic contractor handles the tank, the distribution box and the drain field.

We will not guess whether a backup is the tank or the pipe. If the camera shows a clear house line, the next call should go to a septic service. Ask the DMU if you are unsure whether your street is on sewer or septic.
